# Sources and limits

## Mobbin study
"What good dashboard design looks like: a study of 2,108 dashboards", Rebekah Bek, Mobbin Magazine. https://mobbin.com/blog/what-good-dashboard-design-looks-like
- Sample: 2,108 dashboard-tagged screens from 536 apps, April 2026 snapshot.
- "Engagement" is saves and exports on Mobbin. It shows what designers collect, not end-user behavior.
- Dashboard was 8.9% of free-text searches. 19% of apps use a dashboard as the home screen.
- The page's "nearly double the next term" search claim is not clearly supported by its own chart. Do not repeat it.

## Conference talk
"Onboarding That Clicks", Martin Dabrowski, HelloStavanger 2025. https://youtu.be/10YEl27QREE
- Fogg behavior model (B. J. Fogg, Stanford), COM-B, and the adoption curve are real, established frameworks and are described accurately.
- Numbers (a boss failed by 70% of players, a progress bar starting at 25%) are illustrations with no cited data.
- Critiques of Azure, Google AI Studio, and guided tours are the speaker's experience, not measured results.
- The closing quote is attributed to Nir Eyal. Not verified.
